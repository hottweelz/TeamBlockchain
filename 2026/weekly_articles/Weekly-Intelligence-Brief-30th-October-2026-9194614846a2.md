**Weekly Intelligence Brief 30th October 2026**

**Tokenisation is moving beyond digital ownership records as funds become programmable collateral, stablecoins compete with bank deposits and blockchain wallets enter everyday commerce. Meanwhile, AI agents are beginning to select assets and initiate payments, making identity, delegated authority and liability as important as speed, liquidity and lower transaction costs.**

[**Generali Tokenizes Fund Ownership as AI Meets Programmable Finance**](https://www.generali-investments.com/it/en/institutional/article/generali-investments-launches-its-first-tokenized-fund-class)**.** Generali Investments has launched a natively tokenized share class in its money-market fund, initially restricted to Generali companies. Distributed ledger technology records units and could automate subscriptions, transfers, redemptions and reporting. Generali links the project to its investment in AI and automation, suggesting fund administration will combine intelligent processing with programmable ownership. **Impact on** **Business & Wider Economy:** For other asset managers, this creates pressure to modernise transfer-agency systems, extend dealing hours and make funds usable as collateral or stablecoin reserves. Firms that delay may lose clients to products offering faster settlement, broader distribution and integration with markets. Investors could gain simpler access and quicker redemption proceeds, although Generali’s first deployment remains internal. The wider economy may benefit if tokenised funds release collateral and liquidity efficiently. However, scale requires regulatory consistency, interoperable platforms and legal certainty. The launch proves operational readiness, but not yet market demand.

[**Stablecoin Yields Survive: Crypto Platforms Can Compete with Banks for Deposits**](https://apnews.com/article/stablecoins-circle-usdc-banks-interest-cryptocurrencies-db6b787c7c2a5fe434ce84b23413d966)**.** US regulators have confirmed that cryptocurrency platforms may continue offering interest-like rewards on stablecoin balances, although issuers cannot pay interest. Coinbase, Robinhood and platforms may distribute earnings case by case under rules implementing the GENIUS Act. Coinbase offers up to 3.5% on USDC, compared with an average 0.38% savings rate in many US banks.

**Impact on** **Business & Wider Economy:** The impact could extend far beyond America because dollar stablecoins are accessible internationally. Banks in the UK, Europe and emerging markets may face deposit outflows if customers move cash into higher-yielding USDC, forcing them to raise savings rates, reduce lending or launch competing tokenised deposits. Emerging economies could also experience dollarisation and weaker monetary control. Businesses may gain better returns on operational cash, but stablecoins lack equivalent deposit insurance. Regulators must therefore balance competition and programmable money against cross-border deposit flight, consumer misunderstanding and financial instability.

[**€GOVY Turns Euro Government Bills Into 24-Hour Collateral.**](https://stellar.org/press/archax-announces-eurgovy-on-the-stellar-network-bringing-its-perpetual-tokenised-government-bill-product-to-the-eurozone)Archax has announced €GOVY, a euro-denominated token providing institutions with continuous exposure to short-dated euro-area government bills. The product automatically reinvests proceeds as bills mature, avoiding repeated redemptions and subscriptions, and functions as transferable working collateral. It joins dollar and sterling GOVY versions and will initially operate on Stellar through Archax’s regulated European entity.

**Impact on** **Business & Wider Economy:** For corporate treasurers, €GOVY could turn low-risk liquid assets into programmable collateral available outside conventional market hours. Banks, exchanges and asset managers may use it for settlement, margin and cash-management workflows. The wider economy could benefit if collateral moves faster, reducing idle liquidity and financing costs across euro markets. Yet regulatory treatment will vary by holder, and continuous token transfer does not guarantee continuous underlying-market liquidity. €GOVY illustrates how tokenization may reshape treasury management by joining yield, collateral and payments, rather than merely digitizing ownership records.

[**AI Agents Meet Tokenized Assets: Who Authorizes the Machine?**](https://www.t54.ai/blog/t54-partners-with-arco-financial-agentic-finance-on-xrpl)t54 Labs has signed agreements with RiskX, Hecto Financial and Arco Financial to explore AI-agent payments, structured investments and tokenized-asset workflows. Controls include Know Your Agent verification, checking delegated authority and comparing transactions with limits before execution. Arco would connect tokenized asset allocation with the XRP Ledger AI Hub, while RiskX will examine AI-designed structured products.

**Impact on** **Business & Wider Economy:** For businesses, this addresses a barrier to agentic commerce: proving which machine acted, for whom and within what mandate. Financial institutions could automate transactions without abandoning accountability or compliance evidence. The wider economy may benefit as agents allocate capital, purchase services and manage payments, lowering administrative friction. However, these are exploratory agreements rather than deployed products. If identity, intent and liability remain unresolved, agentic finance may scale fraud as quickly as efficiency. Trust infrastructure, not payment speed, will determine whether machines can transact safely.

[**BlackRock Links Tokenized Funds With Japan’s Yen Stablecoin**](https://startale.com/en/blog/blackrock-startale-sbi)**.** BlackRock, SBI Holdings and Startale will explore connecting tokenized money-market funds with JPYSC, a yen-denominated trust stablecoin. Their work will assess technology, distribution and regulation, combining BlackRock’s asset-management expertise, SBI’s network and Startale’s blockchain infrastructure.

**Impact on** **Business & Wider Economy:** For businesses, the combination could allow idle yen balances to move between payment money and yield-bearing funds without leaving onchain systems, improving treasury flexibility and collateral efficiency. Japanese banks and asset managers may gain products but face pressure to modernize settlement and distribution. The wider economy could benefit from faster capital circulation, greater demand for yen-denominated digital assets and stronger competition with dollar stablecoins. It may reinforce Japan’s role in Asian financial infrastructure. However, the agreement remains exploratory, while legal, liquidity and redemption arrangements must work across institutions. Connecting stablecoins with tokenized funds matters because usable onchain finance requires both transactional cash and productive assets.

[**Mastercard-Backed BVNK Takes Stablecoin Commerce Into Emerging Markets**](https://stellar.org/press/airtm-and-bvnk-partner-to-expand-wallet-native-payments-to-emerging-markets)**.** Airtm and BVNK will enable wallet-native payments in emerging markets through Airtm Connect and BVNK’s stablecoin infrastructure. Users can fund wallets, receive money and pay participating retailers directly, with Stellar providing the blockchain rail. BVNK’s ownership by Mastercard gives Airtm access to broader fiat and stablecoin infrastructure.

**Impact on** **Business & Wider Economy:** For businesses, the integration could open fragmented markets without requiring merchants to manage crypto assets or connect to multiple local providers. Users may gain practical ways to spend dollar-linked balances rather than treating stablecoins only as savings. The wider economy could benefit through cheaper commerce, remittances and digital exports, particularly where local currencies or banking access are weak. However, stablecoin expansion may accelerate dollarization, reduce deposits in domestic banks and complicate monetary policy. The partnership’s commercial value depends on compliant local conversion and widespread merchant acceptance, because blockchain settlement cannot solve last-mile distribution.

[**Bermuda Tests Whether an Entire Economy Can Move Onchain**](https://stellar.org/press/walletconnect-expands-stellar-network-support-and-partnership-with-penrose-partners-to-bring-walletconnect-pay-to-bermuda-in-support-of-the-government-s-onchain-economy-vision)**.** WalletConnect is expanding Stellar support and plans WalletConnect Pay for Bermuda merchants, beginning with Premier Tickets. Customers will pay directly from Stellar wallets to event organizers, so the ticketing platform will not hold client funds. WalletConnect connects hundreds of wallets and institutions, giving merchants a route to digital-asset users.

**Impact on** **Business & Wider Economy:** For businesses, direct wallet payments could reduce custody obligations, speed settlement and provide an alternative to card processing. Bermuda gains a test of its ambition to build an onchain economy rather than limiting blockchain policy to licensing financial companies. The wider economy could benefit if lower payment costs support small merchants, tourism and digital services. However, volatility, refunds, tax reporting and consumer protection must be addressed before adoption spreads. The initiative’s importance lies in testing whether blockchain payments can function within ordinary commerce while regulators, merchants and users share clear responsibilities.
