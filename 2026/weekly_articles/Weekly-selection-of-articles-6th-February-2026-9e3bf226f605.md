### **The Role of Stablecoins in Prediction Markets**

Stablecoins, particularly **USDC**, serve as the fundamental "currency of truth" and settlement for the prediction market ecosystem. Their role includes:

* **Instant, 24/7 Settlement**: Stablecoins enable atomic settlement, allowing winning shares to be paid out the moment an event is verified on-chain, bypassing the delays of traditional banking.
* **Liquidity & Collateralization**: On-chain markets like Polymarket are fully collateralized in stablecoins; the total value of "YES" and "NO" shares always sums to exactly **1 USDC** per pair, ensuring verifiable solvency.
* **Cross-Venue Arbitrage**: Professional desks use stablecoins to execute rapid **relative-value trades** when implied probabilities diverge between regulated venues (like Kalshi) and crypto-native ones (like Polymarket).
* **Programmable Payouts**: Through smart contracts, stablecoins automate complex conditional payouts (e.g., "if X happens, pay Y"), facilitating innovative "Combo" and "Conditional" markets.
* **Global Access**: Stablecoins provide a borderless payment rail, allowing users in regions with unstable local currencies to access dollar-denominated event risk hedging.
