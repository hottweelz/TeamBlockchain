**Weekly articles 17th October 2025**

1. **[Mastercard and Coinbase in battle to buy Bvnk for up to $2.5billion](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/10/09/bvnk-acquisition-coinbase-mastercard-stablecoins/)-** If Coinbase buys Bvnk would then try and move money it controls from Circle? If Mastercard acquires Bvnk it could open it vast global network and could it force VISA to buy a stablecoin? Bvnk is stablecoin infrastructure firm valued between **$1.5–$2.5 billion**, and its acquisition carries far-reaching implications for the payments industry. **1. Strategic consolidation of payments and crypto rails** By acquiring infrastructure that enables **stablecoin-to-fiat conversions and cross-border payments**, Coinbase and Mastercard would fuse conventional payments rails with crypto-native rails. This convergence accelerates the shift toward a unified payments layer underpinned by programmable money. **2. Elevated stakes in stablecoin infrastructure.** As markets mature, control over the plumbing of stablecoin flows becomes as valuable as consumer-facing apps. Ownership of BVNK gives acquirers a structural role in routing liquidity, setting standards, and possibly influencing settlement rules. **3. Pressure on incumbents to adapt**. Traditional banks, card networks, and payment processors face heightened urgency. If crypto platforms anchor financial flows via stablecoins, legacy systems may become bottlenecks or afterthoughts, unless they modernise. **4. Regulatory and compliance tests**. Such a deal would attract intense scrutiny. Stablecoin-related firms must navigate AML/KYC rules, money-transmission licensing, and regulatory clarity (e.g. under the U.S. GENIUS Act). The acquisition would test the new guardrails being built for tokenised finance. **5. Signal of crypto’s financialization.** This move underscores that crypto infrastructure is no longer fringe. It is now part of the **strategic architecture** of financial services. The message to markets: stablecoin middleware is a core battleground. In short, the prospective BVNK acquisition is not just a corporate deal, in time may well prove to bs a keystone event in the offering an alternative for global payments.

[**Major Banks Explore Issuing Stablecoin Pegged to G7 Currencies**](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2025-10-10/major-banks-explore-issuing-stablecoins-pegged-to-g7-currencies) The G7 bank-led stablecoin initiative marks a watershed moment for global finance and potentially a strategic inflection point for every other bank. The consortium’s move to co-develop a regulated, multi-currency stablecoin anchored to G7 currencies positions traditional finance at the heart of digital money infrastructure. For competitors, the implications are profound. First, **the moat between banking and fintech is vanishing**. With major incumbents now embracing tokenised settlement, smaller banks risk being disintermediated from cross-border payments and securities clearing unless they join interoperable networks or adopt tokenisation-as-a-service models. Those relying on correspondent banking for fees and liquidity could see margins erode as programmable settlement replaces manual processing.

Second, **regulatory alignment becomes strategic advantage**. The GENIUS and CLARITY Acts have given U.S.-based institutions a first-mover edge in compliant stablecoin issuance. Banks outside this framework, particularly in the EU under MiCAR may face slower innovation cycles and reduced influence in setting global standards. Third, **data, liquidity, and capital formation will concentrate** around programmable money rails. As tokenised assets move on-chain, stablecoins become the collateral and cash leg of every trade. Regional and mid-tier banks must therefore rethink balance sheet strategy not just lending, but liquidity management in a tokenised economy. [**AI accounts for 40% of US GDP, it had better pay off!**](https://futurism.com/future-society/entire-economy-ai-bubble)**-** The U.S. economy today is underpinned by an extraordinary concentration in artificial intelligence (AI) investments, which now account for about 40% of GDP growth and an outsized 80% of stock market gains in 2025. This level of concentration represents a systemic risk: if AI valuations falter due to a funding pullback or regulatory crackdown, the repercussions could surge through technology sectors, financial markets, and consumer spending alike. This creates a fragility reminiscent of historic bubbles, but with far faster feedback loops due to algorithmic trading and quantitative strategies. The real economy and asset markets have diverged sharply; traditional consumption and industrial productivity flag, even as financial markets soar on speculative investments betting on AI’s future promise rather than immediate economic output. This raises questions about the durability of growth built on inflated valuations. The high concentration of market value in a handful of AI-driven giants can amplify shocks, particularly given passive index funds and exchange-traded funds heavily weighted towards these firms. AI today functions not only as an industry but as an emergent sovereign strategy: control over AI infrastructure, data, and algorithms equates to economic and geopolitical power on par with oil or monetary policy. This elevates AI from a technological trend to an existential economic bet. In sum, the AI boom is no benign wave but a critical juncture. Its sustainability is uncertain, and its potential retreat threatens not only market valuations but also the underlying architecture of modern economies reliant on AI-driven automation and innovation. The real unknow is the significance of the deflationary pressure that AI will bring and while it is likely to impact the jobs market the improved productivity and efficiency in the corporate sector could lead to higher company profits as well as stronger economic growth.

[**BlackRock, the world’s biggest asset manager to build it own tokenisation infrastructure**](https://decrypt.co/344223/blackrock-developing-tokenization-tech-bitcoin-ethereum-etf-success)**.** [The most profitable ETF](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/blackrock-ceo-larry-fink-bitcoin-160319904.html) BlackRock manages is the Bitcoin ETF so little surprise Fink who believes in the tokenisation of everything, to enable AI to offer mass customisation, is now building their own tokenisation platform. BlackRock CEO Larry Fink has reaffirmed the firm’s commitment to tokenisation, describing it as the next frontier in global finance. Speaking after announcing record assets under management of $13.46 trillion, Fink revealed that BlackRock is developing proprietary technology to digitally represent traditional assets, potentially transforming exchange-traded funds (ETFs) into blockchain-based tokens. Such a move could democratise access to capital markets, streamline settlement, and dramatically reduce fees by removing intermediaries. Fink emphasised, “*We need to be tokenizing all assets, especially those with multiple levels of intermediaries*,” adding that tokenization could even lower costs in areas like real estate ownership. BlackRock’s existing tokenization efforts include the **USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund (BUIDL),** one of the world’s largest tokenized funds at **$2.8 billion**—issued via **Securitize**, in which BlackRock invested $47 million earlier this year. Fink noted growing adoption among younger investors and suggested that integrating tokenised products into traditional markets could improve financial literacy and retirement preparedness. BlackRock is positioning itself at the intersection of traditional finance and digital assets. Fink hinted at “***major announcements***” ahead, signalling the firm’s ambition to lead the tokenisation era.

[**Superfast diamond laced chips reduce heat –**](https://www.livescience.com/technology/electronics/superfast-diamond-laced-computer-chips-now-much-closer-to-reality-thanks-to-quantum-breakthrough) Heat is a major problem in silicon chips and it is estimated that Bitcoin miners [convert 95% of their energy use into heat](https://cfc-stmoritz.com/industry-insights/leveraging-bitcoin-mining-to-build-a-more-sustainable-future#:~:text=Recovering%20Data%20Center%20Heat,%2C%20greenhouses%2C%20and%20even%20homes.) so any way to reduce the amount of heat created will make blockchains more environmentally friendly and potentially cheaper to operate. Scientists have made a breakthrough in integrating **diamonds into silicon-based computer chips**, a step that could revolutionise high-performance electronics and quantum computing. Traditionally, diamonds require extreme heat to grow, temperatures incompatible with semiconductor manufacturing. However, researchers at **Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL)** have found a way to drastically reduce the temperature needed for diamond growth using quantum insights. Lower-temperature synthesis means diamonds can now potentially be integrated into standard silicon fabrication, paving the way for **energy-efficient, heat-resistant chips** capable of handling next-generation workloads.

[**Smart money, tokenisation and blockchains will impact financial markets in the future.**](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/speech/2025/october/sarah-breeden-panellist-at-fintech-foundation-2025-dc-fiintech-week) “*Not just token gestures*” − speech by Sarah Breeden, Bank of England. Tokenisation and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) are fundamentally reshaping both retail and wholesale financial services, enabling near-instant settlement, cost efficiencies, and asset fractionalisation. By digitising assets on shared ledgers, they reduce intermediaries, operational risks, and unlock liquidity in historically illiquid markets, expanding access to a wider range of investors and use cases. As programmable smart contracts automate transactions and processes, businesses and consumers gain the potential for more seamless, secure payments and new digital commerce experiences. Central banks and regulators, exemplified by the Bank of England’s Digital Securities Sandbox and RT2 payment infrastructure, are proactively shaping frameworks that permit innovation while maintaining financial stability, systemic resilience, and interoperability between emerging and traditional finance. Stablecoins and tokenised deposits are evolving as settlement assets, with robust regulatory oversight ensuring stability and “singleness of money.” Key risks include operational resilience, legal clarity, and transition challenges, especially if rapid stablecoin adoption shifts deposits from commercial banks. The UK’s strategic, collaborative approach aims to allow market-led experimentation, robust governance, and cross-border harmonisation. Ultimately, tokenisation’s success will depend on responsible industry collaboration, adaptative regulation, and the availability of secure, frictionless forms of digital money, ushering in an era of programmable financial infrastructure with the potential to democratise and future-proof global financial markets

[**DEX’s the digital asset equivalent for dark pools**](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/little-known-crypto-powerhouse-behind-120123529.html) – Traditional securities trading would struggle if it was not for the existence of dark pools. Hyperliquid has rapidly emerged as a major force in crypto derivatives, pioneering decentralised perpetual futures trading with technological agility and real-time transparency. In just two years, its tiny engineering team has built a platform that now processes billions in trading volume, outperforming established players such Coinbase in certain areas and drawing heavyweight backers, including Paradigm and Pantera. Hyperliquid’s innovations, such as its HLP automated liquidity provider vault, offer deep, on-chain liquidity and streamlined order matching, but also concentrate risk and raise governance concerns as the protocol’s foundation controls the bulk of voting power. While praised for speed, openness, and self-custody, critics question its level of true decentralization and potential conflicts of interest. The recent introduction of permissionless market creation and a resilient tokenomics model, where trading fees fuel continuous buybacks—help drive Hyperliquid’s expansion despite volatility and regulatory uncertainty. As the next chapter unfolds, Hyperliquid represents both the promise and complexity of DeFi’s evolution, exposing gaps in regulatory frameworks and illustrating the delicate balance between innovation, market transparency, and robust risk management. DEX’s are set to be a critical part of the digital asset infrastructure are we see more tokenised real-world assets.
