Is MoonPay quietly building the backbone of a new financial order, where stablecoins rival fiat? "*With Iron’s technology, we’re putting the power of instant, programmable payments into the hands of enterprises, FinTechs and global merchants*."

Banks, fearing a deposit exodus that could slash their lending power and profits by up to €8.8 billion annually, are pushing for a €500 cap. But does limiting access to digital euros protect financial stability—or just banks' bottom lines? Meanwhile, the ECB’s own survey raises contradictions: 63% of respondents say they’d use the digital euro, yet 69% claim they’re unlikely to adopt it. Is public demand overstated, or is scepticism simply a byproduct of uncertainty? With the October decision looming, the stakes are rising. Will the digital euro empower consumers by reducing reliance on banks? Or will regulators cave to industry pressure, ensuring it remains a watered-down alternative to cash rather than a transformative force in European finance?

[Nomura tests digital bond issuance settled using tokenized deposits](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/nomura-tests-digital-bond-issuance-settled-using-tokenized-deposits/) - Nomura Research Institute (NRI) recently issued a ¥3 billion ($20 million) digital bond on BOOSTRY's blockchain platform, settling on a T+1 delivery versus payment (DvP) basis using conventional bank money—three days faster than Japan's standard for new issuances. In a separate trial, NRI successfully tested DCJPY, a tokenized deposit digital currency, as a potential alternative to yen for digital bond settlement. ​ These developments highlight a significant shift in Japan's financial landscape, where traditional banking systems are integrating with blockchain technology to enhance efficiency and reduce settlement risks. The successful implementation of DvP in digital bond settlements marks a pivotal move towards more secure and swift financial transactions. Moreover, the exploration of tokenized deposits like DCJPY indicates a readiness to adopt digital currencies for mainstream financial operations, potentially transforming the way securities are settled and paving the way for broader acceptance of digital assets in traditional finance.​ As financial institutions continue to innovate, it becomes imperative to balance technological progress with robust regulatory frameworks to safeguard the stability and integrity of the financial system.
